# Figure it out

## Purpose

Use this fallback only for consequential work that does not fit a narrower playbook or whose scale makes a focused playbook insufficient.
The coordinator must design an auditable workflow before implementing.
Do not reinvent Bug fix, Feature, Refactoring, Performance, Visual parity, Eval, or another existing playbook for a routine unit.

## Completion predicate

The run is complete when its custom falsifiable predicate passes on the real product boundary, every designed phase is settled, the decision trail explains material pivots, and assurance accepts the exact composed revision.

## Step 1: `predicate`

Ground enough of the system to state a falsifiable definition of done.
Quantify scope as rough units, affected owners, expected duration, and known blockers.
Classify reversal cost and blast radius to choose a proportionate rigor level.
Bias toward stronger gates for one-way doors, broad migrations, security boundaries, and unattended work.
Present one checkpoint before a long run, but continue reversible investigation while framing.
Do not encode "done well" as a predicate without observable conditions.

## Step 2: `design`

Design the workflow as ordered phases and record it before implementation.
Put the riskiest unknown, verification harness, and shared foundations before broad feature work.
Compose narrower playbooks and methods rather than reproducing their instructions.
Use Architecture for consequential ownership, Prototype for empirical forks, Arena for independent candidate comparison, and discovery topologies for bounded evidence.
Declare which scopes fan out, their path isolation, dependencies, pilot needs, model roles, and dropout tolerance.
Define product verification, assurance responsibilities, pause points, and the escape condition.
Keep the custom workflow within the structured Workgraph lifecycle rather than inventing mixed node kinds.

## Step 3: `experiment`

Treat each implementation unit as a hypothesis about progress toward the predicate.
Give workers bounded briefs and compose one independently verifiable unit at a time.
Measure the unit on the real or nearest authoritative artifact.
Keep changes that materially advance the predicate and revert or replace changes that do not.
Do not accumulate speculative safeguards, abandoned scaffolding, or duplicate mechanisms.
Use bounded retries chosen from observed failure mode and reconcile uncertain operations first.
If repeated implementation friction contradicts the workflow's architecture, return to design with the new evidence.

## Step 4: `record`

Maintain a compact append-only decision trail for each material phase, experiment, rejection, and scope change.
Link exact commits, artifacts, reports, and sessions instead of embedding their contents.
Record the hypothesis, observation, decision, and effect on the predicate.
Preserve rejected directions when they explain why the accepted design is safer or simpler.
Update playbook step state and explicit skip reasons as the workflow changes.
Keep the trail only as detailed as needed for audit and cold-start resumption.

## Step 5: `verify`

Check the complete predicate against the composed product, not only unit harnesses.
Use independent product verification for unobserved, interactive, visual, performance, or judgment-laden behavior.
Run behavior, structure, and evidence assurance after exact-revision evidence exists.
Have synthesis reject unsupported or duplicate findings and have the coordinator make final dispositions.
Route accepted bounded corrections without restarting settled phases.
Encode a recurring failure lesson in structure or a durable check only when it protects an enduring invariant at acceptable maintenance cost.

## Failure and recovery

A plateau requires a new hypothesis or workflow pivot, not a weaker predicate.
A real dead end requires evidence that credible routes were exhausted within the accepted envelope.
Pause through Pause safely when continuation context cannot remain reliable.
Resume through Session pickup and reconcile before new work.
Ask the user only for an authority-changing choice, irreversible action, unresolvable preference, or genuine capability gap.

## Output

Report the designed workflow, rigor level and reason, decision-trail path, units kept and discarded, exact product evidence, final predicate result, assurance judgment, and remaining unknowns.
