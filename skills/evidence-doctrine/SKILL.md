# Evidence doctrine

Record direct evidence when an observation crosses the required boundary.
Record inference when reasoning connects observations without direct observation.
Record conflicts when supported sources disagree.
Record unknowns when a required fact is unavailable.
For a blast-radius-sensitive change, identify the external safety fact and prove it at the boundary that depends on it.
Never promote inference, absence of conflict, or unavailable tooling into direct evidence.
